# Welcome to 24365.News! 🎥

Congratulations on joining our network of citizen journalists. This guide will help you get started.

## Quick Start

### 1. Complete Your Profile

- Add a display name (how you'll appear on the platform)
- Write a short bio (tell viewers who you are)
- Add your location (city/state helps viewers find local news)
- Link your social media (optional, but helps build your audience)

### 2. Upload Your First Video

1. Click **Upload** in the dashboard
2. Enter a clear, accurate title
3. Write a description with context
4. Select appropriate categories
5. Upload your video file
6. Wait for processing (usually 2-5 minutes)

### 3. Get Verified ✓

Earn a verification badge by:
- Completing your profile
- Publishing 5+ quality videos
- Maintaining good standing for 30 days
- Following our guidelines consistently

## Best Practices

### Before You Record

- **Know the story** — What's happening? Why does it matter?
- **Check your gear** — Battery charged? Storage available?
- **Scout the location** — Find good angles, check lighting
- **Identify yourself** — Have press credentials visible if applicable

### While Recording

- **Landscape mode** for news content
- **Stable footage** — Use a tripod or stabilize against something
- **Clear audio** — Get close to the action, minimize background noise
- **Record context** — Wide shots establish the scene
- **Stay safe** — Don't put yourself in danger for a shot

### After Recording

- **Review footage** — Make sure you got what you need
- **Note details** — Names, times, locations while fresh
- **Back up** — Save copies before uploading
- **Edit thoughtfully** — Remove unnecessary content, but don't alter facts

## What Makes Great Content

### Newsworthy

Ask yourself:
- Is this timely? (Happening now or recently)
- Is it significant? (Affects people's lives)
- Is it interesting? (Compelling story)
- Is it local? (Relevant to a community)

### Well-Produced

- Clear video and audio
- Steady shots
- Good lighting
- Appropriate length (1-10 min for most news)

### Accurate

- Verified facts
- Multiple sources when possible
- Clear attribution
- Corrections when needed

### Engaging

- Strong opening
- Clear narrative
- Human interest elements
- Call to action when appropriate
