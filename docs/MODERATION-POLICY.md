# 24365.News Moderation Policy

## Overview

24365.News is committed to maintaining a platform that serves the public interest while respecting the rights of our contributors and audience. This policy outlines how we moderate content.

## Moderation Approach

### Principles

1. **Minimal Prior Restraint** — We don't pre-approve content, but we review published material
2. **Due Process** — Contributors have the right to understand and appeal decisions
3. **Consistency** — Rules apply equally to all contributors
4. **Transparency** — Our policies are public and clearly communicated
5. **Speed** — We aim to resolve issues quickly

### What We Review

- All newly uploaded videos (within 24 hours)
- Content flagged by users
- Content reported by third parties
- Content identified by automated systems

## Content Categories

### ✅ Approved

Content that meets our guidelines is approved for full distribution.

### ⚠️ Restricted

Content that is legal but sensitive may be:
- Age-restricted
- Excluded from recommendations
- Limited in distribution
- Required to include content warnings

### ❌ Removed

Content that violates our guidelines is removed. Reasons include:
- Misinformation (false claims presented as fact)
- Hate speech
- Harassment or bullying
- Dangerous content
- Illegal content
- Spam or manipulation
- Copyright violations

### 🚫 Strike

Serious violations result in a strike. Three strikes lead to account suspension.

## Process

### Initial Review

1. New content enters review queue
2. Moderator reviews within 24 hours
3. Decision: Approve / Restrict / Remove / Strike

### User Reports

1. User submits report with reason
2. Report reviewed within 48 hours
3. If violation confirmed, appropriate action taken
4. Reporter notified of outcome

### Appeals

1. Contributor notified of decision with reason
2. Contributor can appeal within 14 days
3. Appeal reviewed by different moderator
4. Final decision within 7 days
5. Contributors notified of outcome

## Special Situations

### Breaking News

- Fast-track review for time-sensitive content
- Temporary publication may be allowed pending review
- Accuracy still required — speed doesn't excuse errors

### Newsworthy Content

Content that might otherwise violate guidelines may be permitted if:
- It serves legitimate public interest
- Proper context is provided
- It doesn't glorify harmful behavior
- Alternative approaches were considered

### Government & Political Content

- We don't favor any political party or viewpoint
- We hold all public figures to the same standards
- Political advertising is labeled
- Voter suppression content is prohibited

## Contributor Responsibilities

Contributors agree to:
- Follow Content Guidelines
- Respond to moderation inquiries
- Not attempt to circumvent moderation
- Report violations they observe

## Appeals

### Grounds for Appeal

- Factual error in decision
- Policy was misapplied
- New evidence available
- Extenuating circumstances

### How to Appeal

1. Navigate to the removed content
2. Click "Appeal Decision"
3. Provide explanation and any evidence
4. Submit within 14 days

### Appeal Review

- Reviewed by moderator not involved in original decision
- Decision within 7 days
- Decision is final

## Transparency

We publish:
- Monthly enforcement statistics
- Policy updates and changes
- Notable case studies (anonymized)

## Contact

For moderation questions or concerns:
- Email: moderation@24365.news
- Response time: 48 hours
