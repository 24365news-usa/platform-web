# 24365.News Content Guidelines

## Our Mission

24365.News is a distributed network of citizen journalists committed to covering news 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. We publish content from independent voices across America and beyond, united by a commitment to truth, accuracy, and responsible journalism.

## What We Publish

### ✅ Acceptable Content

- **Breaking News** — First-hand coverage of events as they happen
- **Local News** — City council, community events, local developments
- **Investigative Reports** — Well-researched stories on issues that matter
- **Political Coverage** — Town halls, rallies, press conferences, interviews
- **Weather Events** — Storms, flooding, emergencies
- **Human Interest** — Community stories, profiles, feature content
- **Analysis & Commentary** — Clearly labeled opinion pieces
- **Sports** — Local and national sports coverage

### ❌ Prohibited Content

- **Misinformation** — Deliberately false or misleading content
- **Hate Speech** — Content targeting individuals or groups based on protected characteristics
- **Harassment** — Targeting specific individuals with harmful content
- **Violence** — Gratuitous violence, gore, or content promoting violence
- **Illegal Activity** — Content depicting or promoting illegal acts
- **Spam** — Repetitive, promotional, or off-topic content
- **Plagiarism** — Uncredited use of others' work
- **Private Information** — Doxxing, sharing personal data without consent

## Editorial Standards

### Accuracy

- **Verify before publishing** — Check your facts with multiple sources
- **Correct mistakes promptly** — If you get something wrong, update or retract
- **Distinguish fact from opinion** — Label commentary and analysis clearly
- **Use primary sources** — When possible, go direct to the source

### Attribution

- Always credit sources
- Link to original reporting when citing other outlets
- Get permission before using others' footage or images
- Clearly identify yourself as press when conducting interviews

### Fairness

- Present multiple perspectives on controversial issues
- Give subjects of criticism opportunity to respond
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Disclose relevant affiliations or biases

## Video Quality Standards

### Minimum Requirements

- **Resolution:** 720p minimum, 1080p preferred
- **Audio:** Clear, audible speech; minimal background noise
- **Stability:** Use a tripod or stabilization when possible
- **Lighting:** Subject should be clearly visible

### Recommended Practices

- Film in landscape orientation for news content
- Identify yourself and the date/location at the start
- Keep videos focused — trim unnecessary content
- Add captions for accessibility

## Titles & Descriptions

### Do:
- Write clear, accurate titles that describe the content
- Include relevant details: who, what, when, where
- Use proper capitalization and grammar
- Add complete descriptions with context

### Don't:
- Use clickbait or misleading titles
- Use ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation!!!
- Include unverified claims in titles
- Omit important context

## Categories & Tags

Use appropriate categories:
- Breaking
- Politics
- Local
- Weather
- Sports
- Business
- Health
- Technology
- Entertainment
- Opinion (for commentary)

Add relevant location tags when applicable.

## Copyright & Legal

- Only upload content you created or have rights to
- Obtain releases for interviews when appropriate
- Respect others' intellectual property
- Be aware of shield laws and press protections in your state

## Enforcement

Violations of these guidelines may result in:

1. **Warning** — First-time minor violations
2. **Content Removal** — Content that violates guidelines
3. **Temporary Suspension** — Repeated violations
4. **Permanent Removal** — Serious or persistent violations

Appeals can be submitted via the platform.

Contact the editorial team for guidance on specific situations.
